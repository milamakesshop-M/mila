Short version: Mila doesn’t collect, store or share any of your information. Everything stays in your workbook.

What Mila reads and writes

Mila runs inside Excel, on your device. She reads and changes only the Budget Planner with Mila workbook that is open when you use her, and only to do what you ask (for example, adding a transaction you typed). She has no server, no database and no account system.

What is stored, and where

  • In your workbook: Mila’s settings for that planner (for example, whether daily auto-fill is on, and what “undo” can take back). They are saved with the file, like any cell.
  • On your device: a few preferences in Excel’s local storage for add-ins. They never leave your device.

We use no cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

Files Mila loads

When you open Mila, Excel downloads her code and pictures from https://milamakesshop-m.github.io/mila, the same way a browser loads any web page. That host may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security. Your planner’s contents are never sent there. Like every Office add-in, Mila also loads Microsoft’s add-in library (office.js) from Microsoft; Microsoft’s privacy statement applies to that.

Sharing

We don’t sell, rent or share any information, because we don’t collect any.

Children

Mila is made for adults managing their own money and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
